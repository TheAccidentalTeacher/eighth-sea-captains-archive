The roster

Every ship is a tradeoff.

Figures are inspired by historical classes and the original game’s broad balance. Crownwake remains its own system.

Combat stays coarse.

Sails: sound → damaged → crippled → dismasted.

Hull: sound → battered → leaking → sinking.

Morale: fierce → steady → shaken → surrendering.

Shot is a choice, not homework.

Round punishes hull and guns. Chain destroys sails and masts. Grape cuts crew and morale. The game tracks scarcity only when something has gone seriously wrong.
