The heartbeat

Sail. Fight. Take.

The game gets its speed by revealing one useful layer at a time. Try the encounter, then see how a whole cruise grows from the same handful of decisions.

Ten beats

The complete privateering loop

Optional stories attach to this rhythm. They never replace it.

SightSails on the horizon.
IdentifyClass, strength, then colors.
CommitHail, attack, or run.
ManeuverWind, angle, range.
BroadsideHull, rig, or crew.
BoardClose before the prize sinks.
DuelOne to three sharp exchanges.
PrizeKeep, scuttle, or ransom.
PortRecruit, sell, repair.
RepeatUntil the crew wants shares.
01

Commands have scope

“Attack and take her intact” completes the maneuver, boarding, surrender, and prize assignment without stopping at every submenu.

02

Danger is legible

You hear when the galleon’s broadside could erase your sloop. Cleverness is rewarded; surprise death is not.

03

Story emerges

The captain’s life is the accumulation of prizes, enemies, towns, titles, romances, rescues, losses, and escapes—not a forced quest chain.
