The long game

Every cruise ends. The career remembers.

Your flotilla is temporary. Your fortune, reputation, ranks, relationships, discoveries, protected towns, scars, and enemies are not.

Begin small

A flagship, modest crew, one permanent captain advantage, and whatever reputation survived the last cruise.

Snowball gloriously

Recruit, capture ships, seize cargo, defeat rivals, collect specialists, attack towns, and accumulate unbanked wealth.

Hear the first mate cough politely

Time, hunger, crowding, defeats, and low gold per sailor turn contentment into restlessness and mutiny.

Divide the plunder

Sell unwanted prizes, pay shares, bank personal fortune, keep the chosen flagship, and let most of the expedition disperse.

Begin again—or retire

Your next cruise starts tactically small but historically larger. Retirement weighs the life you actually lived.

Optional pursuits

Filter the life you want.

Named rivals

Hunt famous captains, take their ships, ransom them, earn their hatred, or watch them change sides.

Treasure maps

Assemble landmarks, go ashore, avoid patrols, and search the actual terrain.

Promotions and titles

Serve several powers at once—carefully—and rise from useful rogue to titled menace.

Romance and dancing

Read cues, flourish, recover from mistakes, build alliances, marry, or cause a ball people discuss for years.

Lost people

Pursue captors through taverns, ships, plantations, villages, and clues without cloning a fixed commercial quest.

The Eighth Sea

Brass star-disks, drowned bells, wrong stars, and a passage that may be a route, a place, or a mistake in time.

Retirement is the only scoreboard that matters.

Fortune, fame, rank, titles, land, family, health, discoveries, conquered towns, defeated rivals, and the manner in which people say your name become the ending. If the captain remains healthy and rejects it, the sea can call again.
