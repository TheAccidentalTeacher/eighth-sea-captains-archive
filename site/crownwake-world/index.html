An original archipelago

The Crownwake Main

A warm, violent sea of imperial claims, merchant councils, reef republics, free ports, drowned roads, and charts that disagree with one another.

Select a port on the chart.

The atlas will reveal its starting condition. War and play can change every number.

Four powers

Everyone has a flag. Nobody owns the sea.

Calderan Crown

Fortress ports, silver convoys, disciplined infantry, old nobility, and paperwork magnificent enough to stop a cannonball.

Veyr League

Merchant cities, voting shares, intelligence brokers, fast escorts, excellent credit, and councils divided by profitable principle.

Marrow Kingdom

A naval monarchy short on cash and long on captains. It issues letters of reprisal freely and regrets this on schedule.

Ibis Cantons

Fishing towns, escaped colonies, free farmers, pilots, hidden channels, local loyalty, and grudges with excellent memories.

Independent forces

  • Sounding Guild: pilots whose erased passages appear under heat.
  • Lantern Office: a network investigating wrong stars and impossible arrivals.
  • Mercy Table: surgeons, widows, chaplains, and ransom brokers.
  • Chain Company: conquest performed by debt contract.
  • Freewake Brotherhood: an argument among captains with guns.

The uncanny seam

Eight brass star-disks. A lost survey whose log arrived six months late. Bells that ring underwater before certain storms. A sea reached not by distance, but by taking the correct passage under the wrong stars.

You may ignore all of it and still have a complete career.
