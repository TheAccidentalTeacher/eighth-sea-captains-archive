Six doors in port

No municipal spreadsheet required.

A controlled town delivers benefits and emergencies, not weekly sewer-administration meetings.

1

Governor or council

Wars, pardons, rank, missions, titles, land, prisoners, and allegiance.

2

Tavern

Recruit sailors, buy rumors, find specialists, maps, villains, and opportunities.

3

Merchant

Sell plunder and ships; buy food, repairs, guns, upgrades, or a bargain.

4

Divide plunder

Pay the crew, bank your share, disperse the fleet, and begin a new cruise.

5

Information

Fleet, maps, standings, towns, rumors, captured holdings, and captain’s record.

6

Leave

Back to sea before someone finishes calculating what you owe.

After the walls fall

Choose the town’s future.

Install a power

Give the town to Calderan, Veyr, Marrow, or Ibis rule. Gain one friend and a very motivated enemy.

Restore local rule

Create or restore a council. Loyalty rises; imperial gratitude does not.

Freewake protectorate

Your flag, safe harbor, cheaper repairs, recruits, intelligence, and occasional customs revenue—plus a duty to defend it.

Sack and depart

Take the maximum immediate prize, damage the town’s wealth, and leave someone else to inherit the smoke.
