Specialists

Carpenter, sailmaker, cook, cooper, gunner, surgeon, navigator, and quartermaster create automatic situational advantages.

Ship upgrades

Coppered bottoms, fine canvas, reinforced berths, iron bracing, improved powder, and bronze guns belong to—and can die with—a hull.

Action scenes

Land battles, stealth, treasure searches, dancing, and duels become short readable decisions instead of a single opaque check.

The kitchen-sink rule

An imported feature stays only if it makes a captain’s choice more interesting. It is cut when it becomes bookkeeping, interrupts the hunt–fight–prize rhythm, or forces a central plot.

Official evidence

The remake’s own manual documents multi-ship combat, eight-ship fleets, specialists, upgrades, round/chain/grape shot, land battle, town capture, stealth, dancing, quests, aging, and plunder division.
